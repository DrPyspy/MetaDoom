= QoL Changes =
	- Enemy giblets have been mostly ported into VisualThinkers, putting less strain on performance.

= Weapon Changes =
	- Universal
		- Weapon slots now save your last chosen weapon, allowing you to select your preferred weapons instantly.
		- Added a smoother system for recoil that retains the visual impact without causing the view to hitch.
	- Machine Gun
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
		- Missiles now deviate slightly, veering off course the longer they're flying.
	- Chaingun
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
		- Chaingun alt-fire now heavily slows the player instead of freezing them completely.
	- Grenade Launcher
		- Grenade projectiles now shoot out a little farther.
	- BFG 9000
		- The projectile now shoots tendrils onto more than one enemy as it passes by, dealing more damage overall.
		- Tendril range is longer.
		- Enemies hit by the tendrils will be briefly stunned.
		- The projectile and its tendrils always cause enemies to gib. Delicious!
		- Alt-fire shoots out more projectiles, behaving more similarly to the beta BFG and dealing more consistent damage.

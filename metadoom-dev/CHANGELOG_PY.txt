= QoL Changes =
	- Enemy giblets have been mostly ported into VisualThinkers, putting less strain on performance.

= Weapons =
	- General Changes
		- Weapon slots now save your last chosen weapon, allowing you to select your preferred weapons instantly.
		- Added a smoother system for recoil that retains the visual impact without causing the view to hitch.
		- Super weapons have been moved from slot 7 to 8. A bind to quickly swap to slot 8 has been added for convenience!
		- Quick melee will now use your currently selected melee weapon's alt-fire.
	- Melee Weapons
		- Melee weapons have been generally reworked. Alt-fires require charges.
	- Chainsaw
		- Alt-fire has been reworked.
			- The swipes still rip through enemies, but will only damage them on the first hit.
			- Successful hits spawn health.
			- Killing blows spawn a large bundle of health based on the enemy's initial health.
	- Super Shotgun
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
		- New sounds that play when the hook is on cooldown.
		- Hook cooldown reduced to 4 seconds.
		- Hook is now 'used up' if it has hit an enemy, no more refills if you kill the hooked enemy early.
		- Hook chain has been remade with VisualThinkers! It also looks a bit fancier now, too!
	- Machine Gun
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
		- Missiles now deviate slightly, veering off course the longer they fly.
	- Chaingun
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
		- Chaingun alt-fire now heavily slows the player and prevents jumping, instead of freezing them completely.
	- Rocket Launcher
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
	- Grenade Launcher
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
		- Grenade projectiles now shoot out a little farther.
		- Grenades now bounce more consistently and will always automatically detonate after 1.6s.
	- BFG 9000
		- Sprites have been painted over and revamped!
		- The primary attack has been reworked to lean more into the Doom 3/Doom 2016 behavior. The original tracer-based Doom behavior has been phased out.
			- The projectile now shoots tendrils onto more than one enemy as it passes by, dealing more damage overall.
			- Tendril range is longer.
			- Enemies hit by the tendrils will be briefly stunned.
			- The projectile and its tendrils always cause killed enemies to gib. Delicious!
		- Alt-fire shoots out way more projectiles, behaving more similarly to the beta BFG and dealing more consistent damage. Optimized for close range encounters!
	
= Inventory =
	- General Changes
		- You can now swap between primary and secondary inventory slots, allowing you to quickly access two different inventory items during combat.
		- Inventory cooldown logic has been rewritten and simplified.
	- Flame Belch
		- Added use animation.
		- Flame particles have been redone.
		- Armor drop logic has been moved from the base monster class into the on-fire status itself, allowing DEHACKED monsters to drop armor when set on fire.
	- The Artifact
		- Timestop has been rewritten to hopefully prevent it malfunctioning and not doing anything.
		- Added a fancy shader effect and new sounds to indicate when timestop is active.
		
= Enemies =
	- General Changes
		- Rewrote the monster spawning and randomization system.
	- NEW: Maggot!
		- A quick-moving melee demon that crawls close to the floor to avoid getting hit. Get up close to get a better shot, but watch out for those... hands! Hooves. Nubs?
	- Bull Demon
		- Adjusted spawn chances to appear more often around 10 maps in.
	- Marauder
		- The Marauder has been reworked to be more readable and hopefully more engaging to fight.
		- Now vulnerable to damage from players who aren't his target, letting players team up to take one down.
		- New sprites and animations!
